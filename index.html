Multimodal AI for Autonomous Systems Large Language Models Edge Intelligence Explainable AI

I am an undergraduate researcher applying to PhD programs in Computer Science for Fall 2026. My research focuses on building reliable, interpretable AI systems for autonomous applications, with particular emphasis on multimodal perception–language integration, knowledge-enhanced large language models, and efficient edge deployment.

At the Natural Language Learning Lab (Advisor: Prof. Hyun‑je Song), I work on knowledge graph–enhanced retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures and multi-hop reasoning mechanisms to improve factual accuracy and reasoning capabilities in LLMs. I have also contributed to IoT and edge intelligence systems through international research collaborations.
