AI & Data Analysis

AI-Based Urban Air Impact Analysis System for Factory and Power Plant Emissions (GAIA Project)

This project aims to analyze and predict the urban air quality impact of industrial and power plant emissions using artificial intelligence. Based on seven years (2018–2024) of time-series data collected from the national Tele-Monitoring System (TMS), the team developed a hybrid prediction model combining the AERMOD physical diffusion model with a Transformer-based deep learning architecture.

Transformer AERMOD Time-Series
