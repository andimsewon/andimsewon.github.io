AI-Based Urban Air Impact Analysis System for Factory and Power Plant Emissions (GAIA Project)

Transformer AERMOD Time‑Series Streamlit
Click to view presentation

Analyzes and predicts the urban air‑quality impact of factory and power‑plant emissions using a hybrid AI approach.

Using seven years (2018–2024) of Tele‑Monitoring System (TMS) time‑series data, we combine the AERMOD physical diffusion model with a Transformer‑based deep‑learning architecture.

A data pipeline ingests multi‑source datasets from TMS, the Korea Meteorological Administration, and AirKorea APIs; performs cleaning, gap filling (forward fill, interpolation, LSTM imputation), and temporal feature engineering. The PyTorch Transformer learns multivariate relationships among wind, weather, and pollutants, while a Gaussian Diffusion component models plume‑diffusion uncertainty.

Results are visualized in real time on a Streamlit dashboard, enabling interactive exploration of emission scenarios, 3D plume maps, and observed‑versus‑predicted comparisons. Visualization uses Folium, GeoPandas, and Xarray for spatial heatmaps and time‑lapse animations.

GAIA functions as a data‑driven decision‑support platform for policy planning, industrial strategy, and disaster‑response simulations. Extensions include IoT sensor integration and scaling to nationwide and climate‑change scenarios.

Tech Stack: PyTorch, NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Folium, GeoPandas, Xarray, Streamlit
