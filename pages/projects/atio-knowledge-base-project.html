ATIO Knowledge Base — FAO (UN)

Drupal 10 Solr Milvus Vertex AI

Problem

FAO's Agrifood Technology and Innovation Outlook (ATIO) Knowledge Base brings agrifood innovation records together for policy and innovation teams. Supporting effective knowledge discovery requires understanding how people use the platform and where its interface and AI-enriched content can be improved.

Approach

I contribute within an existing platform built on Drupal 10, where Solr supports structured and faceted search, Milvus supports vector-based semantic similarity, and Gemini via Vertex AI supports AI enrichment. This is the system context I work within; I did not build its retrieval pipeline or vector index.

Result

The active knowledge base contains 5,744 innovation records.

My Role

As an Innovation Accelerator Intern in FAO's Office of Innovation (Jun 2026 – Present, Rome), I contribute to the ATIO Knowledge Base through user engagement and user behavior analysis, UX and data visualization, and evaluation and improvement of AI enrichment prompts. I also collaborate across UX, Drupal implementation, taxonomy development, and data harvesting, while exploring AI-assisted approaches to improve knowledge discovery and user interaction.

System context: Drupal 10, Solr, Milvus, Gemini (Vertex AI)
