ATIO Knowledge Base — FAO (UN)

Drupal 10 Solr Milvus Vertex AI

Problem

FAO's Agrifood Technology and Innovation Outlook (ATIO) knowledge base holds thousands of agrifood innovation records. Keyword search alone makes it hard for policy and innovation teams to find conceptually related records that don't share exact terms.

Approach

Building semantic search and LLM-assisted retrieval on top of the existing Drupal 10 content backend: Solr handles structured/faceted search, Milvus stores vector embeddings for semantic similarity search, and Gemini (via Vertex AI) provides LLM-assisted retrieval and summarization, orchestrated with Python on Google Cloud Platform.

Result

5,744 innovation records indexed. The system is ongoing — an active project at an intergovernmental organization, still in development.

My Role

I am building the semantic search and retrieval pipeline as part of the Innovation Accelerator team at FAO's Office of Innovation.

Tech Stack: Drupal 10, Solr, Milvus, Gemini (Vertex AI), Python, Google Cloud Platform
