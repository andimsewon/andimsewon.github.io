Crop Quality Assessment Using Image Processing and Artificial Intelligence (Guardians of Farm Project)

YOLOv8 Vision Dataset
Click to view presentation

Built an AI system for crop quality assessment in smart‑farm settings, focused on reliable disease detection and efficient leaf‑health evaluation.

We first explored unsupervised visual grounding with GroundingDINO and SAM (Segment Anything Model) for leaf segmentation and RGB‑based classification. Under class imbalance, average‑RGB features proved biased and produced false classifications.

We then constructed a large labeled dataset by combining Kaggle sources with field photos and performed labeling/augmentation in Roboflow. Replacing the pipeline with a YOLOv8‑based supervised detector substantially improved accuracy and inference speed.

The system overlays detections and quantifies crop health into five levels—Normal, Caution, Warning, Danger, Critical—based on the ratio of healthy to diseased leaves. The trained YOLOv8 model achieved mAP@0.5 of 0.81 and AP of 0.846 for diseased leaves, supporting robustness and practical use.

Overall, the project demonstrates a successful transition from unsupervised visual grounding to a high‑performance supervised framework, and it can integrate with IoT pipelines for real‑time monitoring in smart farming.

Tech Stack: Python, PyTorch, YOLOv8, GroundingDINO, SAM, RoboFlow, OpenCV, NumPy
