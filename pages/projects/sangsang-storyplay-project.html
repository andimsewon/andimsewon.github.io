Sangsang StoryPlay – AI-Driven Interactive Storytelling Platform for Children

ASR LLM EdTech
Click to view presentation

A startup-camp concept and prototype plan for child-led interactive storytelling using speech recognition, a generative story engine, and parent-facing activity summaries.

Children would use spoken input to influence the story instead of following a fixed narrative. The proposed interaction flow was:

  • Story interaction: role selection → spoken input → generated scene → next prompt
  • Planned activity analysis: lexical diversity, new-word use, emotional expressions, and branching choices
  • Proposed parent view: summaries of language activity, emotional cues, and preferred story themes

The project materials proposed an ASR/TTS workflow, generative model integration, an application prototype, and cloud-backed interaction history. Multilingual support, smart-speaker integration, and institutional use were documented as possible extensions, not completed deployments.

The completed outputs were the concept, system plan, presentation materials, and startup-camp submission.

Award: Grand Prize (대상), 2025 Jeju National University Tech Business Mode (TBM) Startup Camp
