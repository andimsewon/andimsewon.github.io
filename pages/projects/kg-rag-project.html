KG-RAG: Knowledge-Graph-Augmented Retrieval

PyTorch LangChain RAG

Problem

Standard retrieval-augmented generation retrieves flat text chunks by embedding similarity alone, which gives large language models little structured relational context. That limits factual accuracy and multi-hop reasoning — questions that require chaining facts across multiple connected entities.

Approach

As an undergraduate researcher at the Natural Language Learning Lab, Jeonbuk National University (Feb 2025 – Feb 2026; Advisor: Prof. Hyun-je Song), I designed and implemented knowledge graph–enhanced retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures to improve factual accuracy and multi-hop reasoning in large language models.

  • Developed graph-based retrieval pipelines that integrated structured knowledge with neural embedding spaces using PyTorch and LangChain
  • Implemented multi-hop reasoning mechanisms over knowledge graphs for complex domain-specific question answering, focusing on retrieval precision and model grounding
  • Conducted systematic ablation studies on embedding integration techniques to evaluate accuracy and computational-efficiency trade-offs

Result

The work used multi-hop reasoning over a knowledge graph to improve factual grounding in LLM outputs. A manuscript describing this research, conducted at the Natural Language Learning Lab from Feb 2025 to Feb 2026, is in preparation.

My Role

I designed and implemented the knowledge-graph retrieval pipeline and multi-hop reasoning mechanism as an undergraduate researcher from Feb 2025 to Feb 2026 under Prof. Hyun-je Song.
