Research Experience

My work focuses on knowledge-enhanced large language models, multimodal AI for autonomous systems, and efficient edge deployment. I work at the intersection of NLP, knowledge graphs, and intelligent systems, with emphasis on structured knowledge integration, multi-hop reasoning, and practical deployment considerations.

Research Positions
Undergraduate Researcher
Natural Language Learning Lab | Advisor: Prof. Hyun-je Song
Feb 2025 – Present | Jeonbuk National University, Dept. of Computer Engineering, Jeonju, South Korea
  • Designing and implementing knowledge graph–enhanced retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures to improve factual accuracy and multi-hop reasoning capabilities in large language models
  • Developing graph-based retrieval pipelines that integrate structured knowledge (knowledge graphs) with neural embedding spaces, implemented using PyTorch and LangChain frameworks
  • Implementing multi-hop reasoning mechanisms over knowledge graphs to enable complex query answering in domain-specific applications, with focus on improving retrieval precision and model grounding
  • Conducting systematic ablation studies on embedding integration techniques (entity linking, relation extraction, graph embedding methods) to optimize retrieval performance and evaluate trade-offs between accuracy and computational efficiency
International Research Scholar
Smart Sensor & IoT Project (IITP-Funded)

  • Designed and deployed a LoRa-based distributed IoT sensor network for real-time environmental monitoring in agricultural settings, achieving reliable data transmission over extended ranges with low power consumption
