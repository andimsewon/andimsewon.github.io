Research Experience

My work focuses on knowledge-enhanced large language models, computer vision for field deployment, and efficient edge deployment. I work at the intersection of NLP, knowledge graphs, and intelligent systems, with emphasis on structured knowledge integration, multi-hop reasoning, and practical deployment considerations.

Final-year undergraduate in Computer Science and Engineering at Jeonbuk National University and a data analyst with experience in data analysis/visualization, innovation portfolio documentation, and agrifood technology research. I build reliable, interpretable analytics and end‑to‑end data pipelines for real teams and field contexts.

As an undergraduate researcher at the Natural Language Learning Lab (Feb 2025 – Feb 2026; Advisor: Prof. Hyun‑je Song), I worked on knowledge graph–enhanced retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG) and multi‑hop reasoning to improve factual accuracy and reporting. I also contributed to international, multicultural collaborations in IoT/edge systems and agrifood‑relevant applications.
