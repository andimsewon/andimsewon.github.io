WaggleNet: A LoRa and MQTT-Based Monitoring System for Internal and External Beehive Conditions

LoRa MQTT Edge IoT
Click to view paper

Developed under the Korean AI Square Summer Program at Purdue University, WaggleNet is a cost‑efficient, scalable IoT system for smart apiculture that monitors both internal and external hive conditions.

Most smart‑hive systems track only internal temperature and humidity and often depend on costly GSM/LTE links, which constrain scale and latency. WaggleNet uses Heltec Wi‑Fi LoRa 32 (V3) nodes and a LoRa–MQTT hybrid architecture to capture and transmit ambient and in‑hive conditions in real time.

Each worker node (DHT22, LDR, GPS) samples every three minutes and transmits via LoRa to a master node. The master appends a UTC timestamp and forwards structured JSON to an MQTT server, which writes to a cloud database (SQLite). A Flutter app provides real‑time visualization of temperature, humidity, light intensity, and geolocation, and dispatches threshold‑based alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Field tests at Purdue University (KSW building) and a bee yard near South 525 West, Indiana, confirmed robustness. LoRa links remained stable up to ~110 m in open areas and ~95 m in obstructed environments, including through wooden hive structures.

The system combines modular design, low bill‑of‑materials (≈$10–15 per node), compact hardware, and mobile accessibility—an effective platform for precision apiculture and smart agriculture. Planned extensions include on‑device anomaly detection, hardware miniaturization, and adaptive routing for greater autonomy.

Tech Stack: Hardware: ESP32-S3 (Heltec Wi-Fi LoRa 32 V3), DHT22, LDR, GPS (NEO-6M) | Software: C++, Python, MQTT, SQLite, Flutter, Firebase | Protocols: LoRa (433/868/915 MHz), MQTT Pub/Sub
